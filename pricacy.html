Privacy Policy

How information is handled when you use Kevin Scheduler.

Effective date: October 19, 2026

Before publishing: Review this policy against the app’s actual code, database, hosting, analytics, and Google API configuration. Remove or change any statement that does not accurately describe the service.

1. Who operates the service

Kevin Scheduler is a scheduling application operated by Kevin Okacha / Episteme Ventures (“we,” “us,” or “our”). It allows visitors to select a meeting type, choose an available time, provide meeting details, and schedule a meeting.

Privacy contact: kev.okacha@gmail.com.

2. Information we may collect

Depending on how you use the service, information may include:

  • Booking details: your name, email address, selected meeting type, preferred time, and information you enter in booking or preparation fields.
  • Calendar information: information needed to check availability and create a calendar event, such as event time, title, attendee details, and meeting link.
  • Google account and authorization data: if the operator connects Google Calendar, Google may provide authorization tokens and the calendar data permitted by the scopes approved during setup.
  • Technical information: limited request, device, or log information that may be recorded by the hosting provider for security, reliability, and troubleshooting.

We do not intend to request information that is unnecessary for scheduling. Do not enter sensitive personal information into booking fields unless it is specifically required and you have been told why.

3. How information is used

Information is used to operate the scheduling service, display available meeting times, arrange or manage bookings, send relevant booking communications, create calendar events and meeting links where enabled, respond to requests, protect the service, and troubleshoot technical problems.

4. Google Calendar and Google user data

If the operator authorizes Google Calendar access, the application uses Google data only for the scheduling functions enabled in the app, such as checking availability and creating or managing calendar events.

Google user data is not sold. It is not used for advertising, and it is not transferred to third parties except as needed to provide the requested scheduling functionality, comply with law, protect the service, or as otherwise disclosed and authorized.

Access depends on the permissions granted to the application. You can review or revoke an account’s access through your Google Account’s third-party connections settings. Revoking access may prevent calendar-related features from working.

Our use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will comply with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements where applicable.

5. Sharing and service providers

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers needed to run the application, such as its hosting provider, database provider, Google Calendar, and Google Meet or other meeting services when enabled. Those providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

Information may also be disclosed where required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect rights, safety, and the security of the service.

6. Storage and retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide scheduling, maintain records, resolve disputes, meet legal obligations, and protect the service. Actual retention periods depend on the app’s configuration and operational requirements.

We use reasonable measures intended to protect information, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Your choices and requests

You may contact us to ask about personal information associated with a booking, request correction or deletion where applicable, or ask for help disconnecting Google access. We may need to verify your request and retain some information where the law requires it or where it is necessary for legitimate recordkeeping.

Email requests to kev.okacha@gmail.com.

8. Children’s privacy

The service is not designed for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review the request.

9. International processing

The service and its providers may process or store information in countries other than the country where you live. Privacy protections may differ by location.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the service or its practices change. The effective date at the top indicates when this version was last updated. Material changes will be reflected on this page.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact Kevin Okacha / Episteme Ventures at kev.okacha@gmail.com.
